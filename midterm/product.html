Complete Heritage & Brewing Guide

Santos is renowned as the historic heartland of Brazilian coffee production. Cultivated at elevations ranging from 800 to 1,200 meters above sea level, these coffee cherries benefit from rich, nutrient-dense volcanic soil and ideal microclimates that combine warm sun exposure with cool nighttime mountain breezes.

Our master roasters employ a medium drum-roasting profile specifically calibrated to caramelize sucrose compounds while preserving delicate organic acids. This results in an exceptionally versatile bean that shines equally well in drip coffee makers, pour-over cones, and automated espresso machines.

Post-harvest processing utilizes traditional patio-drying methods where cherries are turned regularly under the warm sun to achieve uniform moisture distribution. This careful post-harvest handling locks in the distinctive chocolate and caramel profile beloved by espresso enthusiasts worldwide.

Recommended Brewing Parameters
  • Brew Ratio: 1:16 coffee-to-water weight (e.g., 15g coffee to 240g water).
  • Water Temperature: 93°C – 95°C (just off a rolling boil).
  • Extraction Time: 3 minutes to 3 minutes 30 seconds for standard pour-overs.
  • Storage: Store in an airtight opaque container away from direct light and moisture.
